Executive Summary

Integrated Africa Company – SUARL is the West African subsidiary of Integrated Company (Saudi Arabia), established in Dakar, Senegal to serve as a strategic distribution gateway for premium Saudi industrial products across the ECOWAS region. We specialize in the authorized supply of SABIC petrochemical polymers, SABIC agricultural fertilizers, and PETROLUBE premium lubricants — connecting world-class Saudi manufacturers with Africa's fastest-growing markets.

SABIC Polymers & Agri-Nutrients

As an approved SABIC distributor, Integrated Africa Company supplies premium petrochemical polymers and agricultural fertilizers to Senegal's manufacturing, packaging, and farming sectors — supporting regional industrial growth with world-class Saudi products.

Petrochemicals

SABIC Polymers

Authorized distribution of SABIC's high-density polyethylene (HDPE F00952) and polypropylene (PP 500GA), essential for packaging, water pipes, agricultural film, and injection molding across West Africa.

  • HDPE F00952 — Blow Molding Grade
  • PP 500GA — Extrusion & Film Grade
  • High Chemical Resistance
  • Superior Tensile Strength
Agriculture

SABIC Agri-Nutrients

Approved supply of SABIC's premium agricultural urea and NPK compound fertilizers, specifically designed to boost crop yields for Senegal's rice paddies, groundnut farms, and horticultural operations.

  • High-Nitrogen Granular Urea (46%)
  • NPK Compound Fertilizers
  • Optimal Soil Enrichment
  • Maximized Crop Productivity

PETROMIN Lubricants & ECOWAS Gateway

Integrated Africa Company delivers premium PETROLUBE industrial lubricants and leverages Dakar's strategic position as the gateway to 15 West African nations, establishing a robust Saudi-to-Senegal supply chain for the entire ECOWAS region.
