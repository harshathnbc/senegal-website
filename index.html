Who We Are

Your Trusted Industrial Partner

Connecting Saudi industrial excellence with West African growth markets.

INTEGRATED AFRICA COMPANY – SUARL

Integrated Company is a premier Saudi corporate delivering top-tier industrial solutions across construction, commercial, and industrial sectors globally. As an approved distributor of world-class petrochemical products and industrial materials, we bridge the gap between Saudi Arabia's manufacturing powerhouse and the rapidly growing markets of West Africa.

Our Senegal branch — INTEGRATED AFRICA COMPANY – SUARL — based in Dakar, serves as the strategic hub for industrial distribution across the ECOWAS region. We specialize in the supply of SABIC polymers and agri-nutrients, alongside PETROLUBE high-performance lubricants and engineering fluids, supporting agricultural expansion, packaging manufacturers, automotive fleets, and heavy industrial operations throughout West Africa.

Our Products

Industrial Products & Distribution

Premium industrial materials from world-leading manufacturers, delivered with robust global supply chain excellence.

Manufacturing

SABIC Polymers

High-density polyethylene (HDPE) and polypropylene (PP) polymers essential for blowing extrusion, injection molding, and agricultural pipe manufacturing. Our SABIC polymer grades — including HDPE F00952 and PP 500GA — are specifically selected for the West African market, supporting packaging manufacturers, water pipe producers, and agricultural film extruders across Senegal and the ECOWAS region.

  • High Tensile Strength
  • Excellent Chemical Resistance
  • Multiple Processing Grades
Agriculture

SABIC Agri-Nutrients

Approved global distributor of SABIC's world-leading agricultural urea and chemical fertilizers, designed to maximize crop yields across diverse soil types. In Senegal, our fertilizers play a critical role in supporting the national agricultural sector — from rice paddies in the Casamance to groundnut farms in the Sine-Saloum — helping local farmers improve food security and economic productivity across the country.

  • High Nitrogen Content
  • Optimized Solubility
  • Soil-Adaptive Formulas
Industrial

PETROLUBE Lubricants

Approved distribution of premium PETROLUBE and NATIONAL heavy-duty automotive engine oils, gear oils, hydraulic fluids, and grease systems. Our lubricant portfolio serves the entire West African market — from mining fleet operators in Guinea to transport companies in Senegal and Mali — providing maximum engine protection, thermal stability, and extended drain intervals across extreme operating conditions.

  • Maximum Wear Protection
  • Thermal Stability
  • Extended Drain Intervals
Global Reach

ECOWAS Market Gateway

Senegal serves as our strategic gateway to the entire ECOWAS economic region. We are actively expanding our supply footprint across 15 West African nations, building robust logistics pipelines from Saudi Arabia through Dakar to serve growing industrial demands in construction, agriculture, and transportation sectors.

  • 15 ECOWAS Nations
  • Saudi-Dakar Supply Chain
